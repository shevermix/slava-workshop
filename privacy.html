Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Slavik Workshop (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates this website to offer kitchen cabinet painting services in the Boston, Massachusetts area. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the website and how we use it. By using this website, you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

We only collect the information you choose to give us through our contact / free estimate form, which may include:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Any details or photos you include when you contact us

We do not use advertising trackers, analytics pixels, or marketing cookies on this website, and we do not set cookies of our own.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you submit solely to:

  • Respond to your request and provide a free estimate
  • Contact you about your project and our services
  • Schedule and carry out work you request

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

How Your Information Is Handled

When you submit the form, your details are delivered to us by email through a third-party form service (FormSubmit). The website is hosted on Netlify. These providers process your data only to deliver your message to us.

Third-Party Content

Some pages load content from third parties, which may receive your IP address as a normal part of delivering that content:

  • Google Fonts (typefaces)
  • Map tiles for our service-area map
  • YouTube (privacy-enhanced mode) for project videos — loaded only if you click to play a video
  • Links to our Instagram profile

Data Retention

We keep the messages and estimate requests you send us for as long as needed to serve you and maintain our business records. You may ask us to delete your information at any time using the contact details below.

Your Choices

You can contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have provided. Since we collect information only when you submit it voluntarily, you can also simply choose not to submit the form.

Children’s Privacy

This website is intended for adults seeking home improvement services and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
